### About the Experiment 

The objective of the "Parasitoid-Host Dynamics" experiment is to simulate and understand the complex interactions between parasitoids and their hosts in an ecological setting. The experiment models the dynamics of parasitism, demonstrating how parasitoid populations impact host populations over time, and how various factors such as host resistance, parasitoid reproduction rates, and environmental conditions influence these interactions. Through this virtual lab simulation, students and researchers can explore theoretical and practical aspects of population dynamics, contributing to a deeper understanding of biological control mechanisms, evolutionary strategies, and ecological balance.
